What the options do

Write skill
The candidate is a good idea — have the writer agent draft the skill. You review the draft next.
Accept
Put it to work: a skill is installed into your agents' skill directories and the library; a memory or rule becomes active in the library at the chosen scope.
Rewrite…
Ask the writer for a new draft, with an optional note to steer it (what was wrong, what to focus on). The note is kept for the next rewrite.
Reject
No — and don't propose it again. Your reason is remembered (and syncs between machines).
Verify
The entry is still true: resets the staleness clock without needing a use.
Pin / Unpin
Pinned entries are never archived by decay; unpin lets them age again.
Archive
Stop using an entry (soft delete): it leaves the digest and your agents, but stays restorable. Archiving a skill also removes it from your agents' skill directories.
Restore
Bring an archived entry back into use; this also refreshes its staleness clock.
Decay
Entries nobody used or verified for a while are archived automatically. Each one gets a card in the queue to restore or confirm.
